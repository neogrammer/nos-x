NOS-X privacy policy

In effect from 22 November 2026.

The short version: NOS-X does not collect, store or share any personal information about you. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it works without an internet connection.

Who we are

NOS-X is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest headsets, made and published by BB Ink, an independent developer. "We" and "us" on this page mean BB Ink. You can reach us at j2thahardee@gmail.com.

What information the game collects

None. NOS-X does not ask for your name, your email address or any other personal information, and it does not collect any in the background. In particular:

  • There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.
  • There are no ads and no in-game purchases.
  • There is no analytics or tracking software in the game.
  • The game has no online features and does not send information about you or your play to us or to anyone else.
  • The game does not use the microphone, the cameras' pictures, your location, your contacts or your files.

What the game keeps on your headset

So that you can carry on where you left off, the game saves your progress on your own headset: your three save files (the stages you have beaten, the weapons, upgrades and items you have found, and your best times), your options and your best score. This stays on your headset. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it.

Hand and head tracking

NOS-X is played with your hands or with controllers. While you play, the game reads the position of your head and hands from the headset so that it can move your view, your arm cannon and your blade. This happens in the moment, on your headset. The game does not record it, keep it or send it anywhere.

How information is used and shared

Since the game collects no personal information, there is none for us to use, sell or share with anyone.

Meta and the store

You get NOS-X through the Meta Horizon Store and play it on a Meta Quest headset. Meta runs the store and the headset's system software, and handles your Meta account, your purchase and any review you choose to post. What Meta collects when you use its store and its headsets is covered by Meta's own privacy policy, not by this one. Meta shows developers overall figures, such as how many people have bought a game, and the public reviews people post. These figures do not tell us who you are.

Deleting your data

Everything the game saves is on your headset and under your control:

  • To delete one save file, choose it on the game's save file board and pick the delete option.
  • To delete everything the game has saved, uninstall NOS-X from your headset.

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing on our side to delete. If you have any question about your data, or you want to make a request about it, write to j2thahardee@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

NOS-X is meant for players aged 13 and over. The game collects no personal information from anyone, whatever their age.

If you write to us

If you email us, we see your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you, and we do not pass it on.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no tracking of its own. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details of visits, such as your IP address, as its own privacy statement describes.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
