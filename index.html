What it is

NOS-X is a seated VR action game for Meta Quest. Instead of watching a hero run from left to right, you are the one running, down a narrow lane and straight into the stage. You only ever go forward or back. Everything else is jumping, dashing, kicking off walls, climbing ladders, shooting and cutting.

The name comes from nostalgia: tight jumps, a stage select you can take in any order, and bosses whose weapons become yours.

What is in it

13 stages

An intro, eight stages you can take in any order, and four final ones. Each has a world of its own, from a reactor hall to a neon city.

12 bosses, 8 weapons

Beat a boss and its weapon is yours. Every weapon is another boss's weakness, so the order you choose matters.

Played with bare hands

Hold a hand out to run, pinch to jump, dash and shoot, swing the blade to cut and block. Controllers work too, if you prefer them.

Made for sitting down

The whole game is played seated. Your view stays steady, and you turn round with a quick half turn.

Secrets worth coming back for

Five of the weapons open hidden gates in other stages. Behind them are four upgrades, four life tanks, heart crystals and extra lives.

A guide when you are stuck

TINBEARD, a robot wizard, steps out of a portal with a tip, a riddle or a light to show the way on.
